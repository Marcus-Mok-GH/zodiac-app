## Quick Deploy to Vercel

Click the button below to deploy directly to Vercel:

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FMarcus-Mok-GH%2Fzodiac-app&project-name=zodiac-app&repository-name=zodiac-app)

Or manually deploy:

1. Go to https://vercel.com/new
2. Select "Import Git Repository"
3. Paste: `https://github.com/Marcus-Mok-GH/zodiac-app`
4. Click "Import"
5. Click "Deploy"

Your app will be live in ~2 minutes!
