# Zodiac App Deployment to Vercel

## Quick Deploy

Click the button below to deploy directly to Vercel:

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/Marcus-Mok-GH/zodiac-app)

## Manual Steps

1. Go to [https://vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new)
2. Click "Continue with GitHub"
3. Search for `zodiac-app` repository
4. Click "Import"
5. Accept the default settings and click "Deploy"
6. Your app will be live in ~1-2 minutes!

## App Features

- **Zodiac Sign Calculator**: Enter your birth date to discover your zodiac sign
- **Detailed Information**: Get the zodiac symbol, date range, and element
- **Beautiful UI**: Modern gradient design with smooth animations
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on mobile and desktop

## Tech Stack

- Next.js 14
- React
- Tailwind CSS
- JavaScript

Your deployed app will be available at: `https://zodiac-app.vercel.app`
